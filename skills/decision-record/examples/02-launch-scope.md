# Example: launch-scope decision

Context:
The team plans to launch a new research workspace next month. The original scope included saved searches, shared folders, annotations, permissions, notifications, and AI summaries.

Discussion:
User testing showed the strongest need is quickly returning to useful research and sharing a small collection with colleagues. Notifications were not tested. AI summaries depend on a search-quality improvement that is not complete.

Options discussed:
1. Delay launch until every planned capability is ready.
2. Launch a focused first version with saving, simple sharing, and source links.
3. Launch everything except AI summaries.

Decision:
Launch a focused first version with saving, simple sharing, and source links. Defer notifications and AI summaries.

Reasoning:
The smaller release addresses the clearest validated need and avoids coupling launch to unfinished AI work.

Owner:
Head of Product

Next action:
Design and engineering to revise scope and confirm what can be released safely next month.
