# Example: unresolved decision

Context:
The team is considering whether to make the new reporting feature available to all customers or only enterprise plans.

Discussion:
Sales believes broader access will help conversion. Customer Success is concerned that self-serve customers may need more onboarding. Product analytics has not yet shown whether report usage differs meaningfully by plan.

No final decision was made.

Open questions:
- What support burden would self-serve availability create?
- Is reporting a meaningful enterprise-plan differentiator?
- Can the team support a staged rollout by customer segment?

Next action:
Product Operations to gather plan-level usage data and Customer Success feedback before the next pricing meeting.
