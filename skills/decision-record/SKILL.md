# Decision Record

Create a durable, decision-useful record from the supplied context.

Capture what was decided, why, which alternatives were considered, what is still uncertain, and what must happen next. Do not invent agreement, rationale, owners, or evidence that the source material does not support.

## Operating rules

- Distinguish a decision from a proposal, preference, action item, or unresolved discussion.
- State “No decision yet” when the supplied context does not establish one.
- Preserve meaningful dissent, uncertainty, and conditions.
- Separate facts, assumptions, constraints, and rationale.
- Do not rewrite uncertainty into false confidence.
- Keep alternatives that were genuinely considered; do not fabricate a long option list.
- Record trade-offs honestly. Every meaningful choice excludes something.
- Prefer named owners when supplied; otherwise use “to assign”.
- Keep the record concise enough to be useful later.

## Analyse

Identify:

1. The decision being made
2. Its context and trigger
3. The selected option, if one exists
4. Alternatives considered
5. Evidence, constraints, and rationale
6. Trade-offs, risks, and consequences
7. Open questions or conditions
8. Owners, next actions, and review date

## Response format

Use this exact structure.

# Decision: [short, outcome-based title]

**Status:** Decided / Proposed / No decision yet  
**Date:** [use supplied date or “Not supplied”]  
**Owner:** [name, role, or “to assign”]  
**Review date:** [date, trigger, or “Not yet defined”]

## Context

Write 2–4 sentences explaining why this decision is needed now.

## Decision

State the chosen option plainly. If no decision has been made, state the decision that remains open and what is blocking it.

## Rationale

List only rationale supported by the supplied context.

- [Reason]

## Options considered

### [Option]

- **Outcome**: [what this option would mean]
- **Benefits**: [supported benefits]
- **Trade-offs**: [supported costs, risks, or drawbacks]
- **Disposition**: Chosen / Rejected / Deferred / Still open

## Consequences and risks

List the material consequences of the decision.

- [Consequence or risk]: [what needs to be managed]

## Open questions and assumptions

- [Question or assumption]
  - Why it matters: [specific consequence]
  - Owner: [name, role, or “to assign”]

## Next actions

- [Action] — **Owner:** [owner] — **By:** [date or “Not specified”]
