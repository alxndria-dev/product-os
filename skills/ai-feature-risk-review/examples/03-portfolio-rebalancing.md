# Example: portfolio-rebalancing recommendations

Product / feature:
Automatic portfolio-rebalancing recommendations

Who it is for:
Retail investors using our investment platform.

Problem:
Some customers are unsure when their portfolio has drifted away from their intended allocation.

Proposed AI behaviour:
The system analyses each customer’s investments, identifies diversification issues, and recommends trades. A future version may allow customers to automatically accept recommended trades.

Inputs and data sources:
Portfolio holdings, stated risk preference, transaction history, market data, and third-party security information.

Output or action:
A recommendation to buy or sell specific assets, with projected changes to allocation.

Evidence:
Support receives questions about diversification. A competitor has launched an AI investing assistant.

Success looks like:
More customers use portfolio tools and assets under management increase.

Constraints:
We operate in regulated markets and use third-party market data.
