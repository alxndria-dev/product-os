# Example: research answer assistant

Product / feature:
Research Answer Assistant

Who it is for:
Investment analysts using our internal research platform.

Problem:
Analysts spend time searching across internal reports and company documents to answer routine questions about companies, sectors, and market events.

Proposed AI behaviour:
A user asks a question in natural language. The assistant searches approved internal research, produces a concise answer, and suggests related reports.

Inputs and data sources:
Internal analyst reports, licensed market-data summaries, and company documents. Some material may be several months old.

Output or action:
A written answer in the product. The user can open suggested source reports.

Evidence:
Analysts frequently search for similar questions and switch between several systems to find prior research.

Success looks like:
Analysts find relevant evidence faster and perform fewer repeated searches.

Constraints:
The tool must not present stale information as current or expose research a user is not permitted to access.
