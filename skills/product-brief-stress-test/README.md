# Product Brief Stress-Test

A structured review for rough product or feature briefs.

Use it before committing to discovery, design, or delivery when a request may be solution-led, under-evidenced, or missing key decisions.

## Input

Provide as much of the following as you have:

```md
Product / feature:
Who it is for:
Problem:
Proposed solution:
Evidence:
Success looks like:
