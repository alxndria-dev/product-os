# Example: solution-first brief

Product / feature:
AI Insights Dashboard

Who it is for:
Our customers

Problem:
Customers need to understand their business data better.

Proposed solution:
A dashboard that uses AI to identify trends, risks, and opportunities across customer data. It should feel modern and use natural language summaries.

Evidence:
Several people have said reporting is difficult. Competitors have AI features.

Success looks like:
Higher engagement and customers seeing more value in the platform.

Constraints:
It needs to be ready for the next quarterly release.
