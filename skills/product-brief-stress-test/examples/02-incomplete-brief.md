# Example: credible but incomplete brief

Product / feature:
Saved research workspace

Who it is for:
Investment analysts using our research platform several times each week.

Problem:
Analysts find useful company research, but cannot easily return to it, organise it by topic, or share a selection with colleagues. They often save browser tabs, copy links into notes, or repeat searches.

Proposed solution:
Allow users to save research results into named workspaces, add notes and tags, then share a workspace with teammates.

Evidence:
Five customer calls included complaints about repeated searches and fragmented personal note-taking. Product analytics show users frequently revisit the same company searches.

Success looks like:
Analysts spend less time finding previously viewed research and share research more often.

Constraints:
The first release must work with current search-result data and existing workspace permissions.
