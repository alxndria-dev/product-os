# Example: mixed evidence

Research question:
Should the product add an AI-generated weekly market summary?

Customer interview, active user:
“I would read a summary if it linked to the original research. I would not trust it on its own.”

Customer interview, active user:
“I already have too many newsletters. I only care when something changes in the companies I follow.”

Customer interview, infrequent user:
“A weekly digest might remind me to come back, but I would probably skim it.”

Support-ticket review:
Seven customers asked where to find recent research on specific companies. None asked for a weekly summary.

Product analytics:
The most engaged users revisit followed companies after market events. Weekly email engagement is not currently available.

Sales feedback:
Two prospects said they wanted “AI summaries,” but no further detail was recorded.
