# Example: small sample

Research question:
Would recruiters benefit from an automatic candidate-ranking feature?

Participant 1, agency recruiter:
“If it saves time on obvious mismatches, I am interested. But I need to understand why it ranked someone lower.”

Participant 2, in-house recruiter:
“We cannot use a black-box ranking tool. Hiring managers would challenge it and I would have no answer.”

Participant 3, agency recruiter:
“Ranking is not the problem. We get poor briefs from hiring managers, so the search starts wrong.”

Observation:
All three participants asked questions about explainability or control. Two were concerned about trust; one challenged the framing of the problem itself.

Study limitation:
Only three participants were interviewed. Two worked in agencies.
