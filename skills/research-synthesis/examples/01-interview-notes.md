# Example: research interviews

Research question:
How do investment analysts currently save, return to, and share useful research?

Participant 1, senior analyst:
“I usually search again because I cannot remember where I saw something. I have browser tabs, notes, and saved links everywhere.”

Participant 2, associate analyst:
“I save the link in OneNote, but that only works for me. If someone asks where the research came from, I have to find it again.”

Participant 3, portfolio manager:
“I do not want another place to organise documents. I need to know whether the research is current and who has already looked at it.”

Participant 4, analyst:
“I would use folders if they were quick, but I do not have time to name and maintain everything.”

Observation:
All four participants moved between the research platform and an external note-taking or bookmarking tool.
