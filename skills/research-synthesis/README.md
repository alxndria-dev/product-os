# Research Synthesis

An evidence-led workflow for turning messy customer research into themes, confidence levels, opportunities, and practical next steps.

Use it after interviews, usability tests, support-ticket reviews, surveys, sales calls, or mixed discovery material.

## Input

Provide raw or lightly structured material such as:

- Interview notes or transcripts
- Direct quotes
- Usability-test observations
- Support tickets
- Survey responses
- Product analytics or behavioural observations
- A research question or decision, if one exists

The skill works with incomplete evidence. It should name the limitations rather than pretending the sample is representative.

## Output

The synthesis returns:

1. Research scope and limitations
2. Three to five evidence-backed themes
3. Tensions and counter-evidence
4. Opportunities or questions to explore
5. One recommended next step

## Confidence levels

- **High**: Consistent, direct evidence across a suitable range of relevant participants or data.
- **Medium**: A meaningful pattern exists, but coverage or context is limited.
- **Low**: An early signal worth exploring, not a basis for a major decision.

## What it does not do

- Turn a few quotes into universal customer truth
- Hide conflicting evidence
- Invent participant motivations or missing context
- Recommend a feature merely because users asked for one
- Replace formal research planning, statistical analysis, or specialist research judgement
